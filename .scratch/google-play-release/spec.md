# First Google Play release (Android)

## Problem Statement

MobTT has never been published. The Play developer account is being re-enabled, so Play Console steps are blocked. Everything that can be prepared offline should be ready, so the release is a matter of filling forms and uploading.

## Solution

Release signing from untracked settings, a minified reproducible `prod` bundle, store texts and graphics in English and German, drafts of the privacy policy, and a checklist that separates repo work from Play Console work. iOS is out of scope (no Apple Developer account).

## Out of scope

iOS, tablet screenshots, the Fixture link domain (ADR-0011), enabling Fixture sharing in `prod`.

See `docs/RELEASE_ANDROID.md`, `docs/PLAY_CONSOLE_CHECKLIST.md` and `store/google-play/README.md`.
