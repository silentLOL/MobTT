## The short version

MobTT has no accounts and no ads. Your choices (My Player, My Club, Favorites, appearance) stay on your device. If, and only if, you tap "Allow" on the consent step, MobTT sends anonymous usage statistics and crash reports to Google Firebase. You can turn this off in Settings at any time.

## What stays on your device

My Player, My Club, Favorites, the appearance setting and your consent choice are stored locally by the app. They are not sent to us or to anyone else.

## Usage statistics and crash reports (only with your consent)

With your consent, MobTT uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics, provided by Google. They receive:

- which screens you open and a few interactions (for example that a Favorite was added, a search was made, or a detail screen was opened), never which Player, Club or Team it concerns;
- the length of a search text, never the text itself;
- crash reports with technical device information (device model, Android version, app version, stack trace);
- an app installation identifier that Firebase generates. It is not your advertising ID; MobTT does not use the advertising ID.

Nothing is sent before you consent. If you decline or withdraw consent, collection stops. The legal basis is your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR). Firebase data is processed by Google as our service provider; Google may process it outside the EU under its standard contractual terms. Firebase keeps event data for [2 / 14] months and crash data for 90 days, depending on the setting in our Firebase project. We use the data to fix bugs and understand which features are used.

## Data the app fetches

To show results the app requests public data from the XTTV results service (`oettv.xttv.at`) and, for a Player's Rating history, from Ratings Central (`www.ratingscentral.com`). These requests contain the identifiers of what you open (for example a Player), the app name and version, and, like every internet request, your IP address, which those services can see. MobTT does not send your name or any account detail. Please see their own privacy information for how they treat requests.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you may ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction and portability of personal data, and you may withdraw consent at any time (Settings > Usage statistics and crash reports). Because usage data is tied only to an anonymous installation identifier, we cannot identify you in it. You may complain to the Austrian data protection authority (dsb.gv.at).
